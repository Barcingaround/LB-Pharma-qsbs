### Cerevel Therapeutics (CERE)
- **Deal Status:** ✅ CONFIRMED
- **Price per share:** $45.00 cash
- **Total equity value:** $8.7 billion
- **Announcement date:** December 6, 2023
- **Completion date:** August 1, 2024
- **Acquirer:** AbbVie
- **Premium:** 22% premium to prior closing price
- **Key asset:** Emraclidine (M4 PAM for schizophrenia)
- **⚠️ CRITICAL UPDATE - Emraclidine Phase 2 FAILED:**
  - **Date:** November 11, 2024
  - **Results:** Both EMPOWER-1 and EMPOWER-2 Phase 2 trials did NOT meet primary endpoint
  - **Outcome:** Failed to show statistically significant reduction in PANSS total score vs placebo at week 6
  - **Financial impact:** $3.5 billion impairment charge for AbbVie; stock dropped 12%
  - **Current status:** AbbVie pivoting to adjunct therapy approach; advancing Alzheimer's and Parkinson's programs instead
  - **Implication for valuation:** This comp should be heavily discounted or potentially removed from primary analysis due to post-acquisition clinical failure
- **Sources:**
  - [AbbVie Acquisition Announcement](https://news.abbvie.com/2023-12-06-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Cerevel-Therapeutics-in-Transformative-Transaction-to-Strengthen-Neuroscience-Pipeline)

### Key Takeaways for Valuation Model

1. **Strong precedent for CNS psychiatry deals:** Three major deals ($8.7B-$14.6B) in 24-month window confirms robust M&A market
2. **Critical risk factor:** Cerevel/emraclidine failure demonstrates clinical risk can destroy value post-acquisition
3. **Public market comps range:** $4.3B (ACAD) to $12.3B (NBIX) to $9.4B (AXSM) - wide range based on pipeline stage and commercial traction
4. **Deal premium context:** Premiums ranged from 22% (Cerevel) to 53% (Karuna) to 39% (ITCI)
5. **Market momentum:** CNS/neuroscience overtook oncology in 2025 deal value - strong strategic interest continues into 2026

### Valuation Modeling Guidance

**Recommended comp weighting:**
- **Heavy weight:** Karuna ($14B) and ITCI ($14.6B) - both successful, completed/announced deals
- **Moderate weight:** Public comps (NBIX, ACAD, AXSM) for reality check on market valuations
- **Light/zero weight:** Cerevel - clinical failure post-acquisition undermines precedent value

**For Phase 2 vs. Phase 3 assets:** Note that ITCI had an FDA-approved drug (CAPLYTA), while Karuna's KarXT was under FDA review. If target asset is earlier stage (Phase 2), apply appropriate discount vs. these late-stage/commercial comps.
